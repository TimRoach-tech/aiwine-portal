1 Agreement

This agreement is between AIWine Ventures Limited ("AIWine", "we", "us") and the winery or wine producer ("you"). By accepting these terms and listing wines on AIWine, you confirm that you are authorised to enter into this agreement on behalf of your business.

AIWine provides the online marketplace, customer ordering platform and payment processing. You remain responsible for your wines, their quality, pricing, fulfilment and compliance with New Zealand law.

2 Listing your wines

You may only list wines that you are legally entitled to sell in New Zealand. You are responsible for ensuring all information you provide is accurate and kept up to date, including:

  • wine name and vintage;
  • variety;
  • tasting notes;
  • alcohol content;
  • pricing;
  • stock availability;
  • product images.
AI-powered recommendations. AIWine uses the information you provide to generate AI-powered wine recommendations, search results and product descriptions. These recommendations are generated automatically to help customers discover suitable wines. While AIWine aims to provide accurate and relevant recommendations, they do not constitute professional advice or a guarantee that a wine will meet a customer's personal preferences or expectations.

3 Pricing & availability

You set your own retail prices. Customers are charged the price displayed at checkout. You agree to honour accepted orders at that price and keep stock levels up to date. If you cannot fulfil an order, you must notify AIWine immediately.

AIWine reserves the right to cancel and refund any order where a wine is unavailable, incorrectly priced due to an obvious error, or cannot reasonably be fulfilled.

4 Orders & fulfilment

All wines are packed and dispatched directly by the winery. You are responsible for:

  • packing orders safely;
  • dispatching orders promptly;
  • updating order status in the Winery Portal;
  • complying with all delivery and age-verification requirements.

Every order reflects both your winery and AIWine. We ask that all orders are packed carefully, dispatched promptly and delivered to the standard customers expect.

5 Payments

Customers pay AIWine at checkout. AIWine deducts the agreed commission (plus GST where applicable) and pays the remaining balance to you during the first week of the following month. Current commission rates and payment schedules are published in the Winery Portal.

Outstanding customer orders must still be fulfilled unless otherwise agreed.
