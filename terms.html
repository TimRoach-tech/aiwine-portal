1 Who these terms are between

This agreement is between AIWine Limited ("AIWine", "we", "us"), the operator of the AIWine marketplace and winery portal, and you, the winery or wine producer listing wine for sale ("you", "the winery"). By ticking the agreement box in the portal and uploading wine, an authorised representative of the winery accepts these terms on the winery's behalf.

2 Listing your wine

The portal lets you publish your range to the AIWine marketplace. You may list only wine that you are legally entitled to sell in New Zealand and that you produce, own, or are authorised to distribute. Each listing should represent a genuine, available product.

3 Accuracy & responsibility

You are responsible for the accuracy of everything you upload, including wine names, varieties, vintages, tasting notes, alcohol content, prices, and stock levels. You agree that:

  • listing information is truthful and not misleading;
  • images you provide are of the actual product and that you hold the rights to use them;
  • you will keep stock and pricing reasonably up to date.
AIWine's AI sommelier only ever recommends wines that are genuinely in your listed inventory, at the price you set. Keeping your data accurate is what makes that promise true for your customers.

4 Pricing, stock & availability

You set the retail price of each wine. The price shown to customers, and the price charged at checkout, is the price in your listing at the time of purchase. You are responsible for honouring listed prices and for keeping stock levels accurate so that customers are not sold wine you cannot supply.

5 Orders & fulfilment

Wine on AIWine ships direct from you to the customer. When an order is placed you agree to:

  • pack and dispatch the wine promptly, in line with any fulfilment settings you have configured;
  • ship to the customer's stated delivery address using a carrier appropriate for alcohol;
  • update the order status in the portal so the customer is kept informed.

You are responsible for safe packaging and for delivery to the customer.

6 Commission & payment

AIWine charges a commission on each completed sale, as set out in your plan and the portal's Payments section. The customer pays AIWine at checkout; AIWine deducts commission (plus GST on that commission) and remits the balance to you. Payouts are made monthly, in the first week of the following month, to the bank account you provide. Full details and a payouts schedule are shown in the portal's Payments section.

7 Tax & invoicing

You are responsible for your own tax obligations on the sale of your wine, including GST where applicable. AIWine issues four documents per settlement — an order confirmation, a commission tax invoice, a sales statement, and a remittance advice — to keep your records GST-correct. You must provide a valid GST number (if registered) before your first payout so these documents are accurate.

8 Alcohol law compliance

You warrant that you hold all licences required to sell and dispatch alcohol in New Zealand, and that you will comply with the Sale and Supply of Alcohol Act 2012 and all related obligations, including not supplying to minors. You are responsible for age-verification obligations on dispatch. AIWine displays age-restriction notices to customers but does not remove your legal responsibilities as the seller of record for your wine.

You retain ownership of your brand, wine names, images, and descriptions. You grant AIWine a non-exclusive licence to display and promote that content on the marketplace and in related marketing while your wines are listed. You can withdraw a wine at any time, and we will stop displaying it.

10 Data & privacy

11 Suspension & removal

We may suspend or remove a listing, or a winery account, if information is materially inaccurate, if orders are repeatedly unfulfilled, or if these terms or applicable law are breached. Where practical we will contact you first so issues can be put right.
